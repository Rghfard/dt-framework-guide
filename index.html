Illustrative replenishment application

Consider an incoming replenishment shipment whose expected arrival is delayed. Its operational significance depends on destination inventory, expected demand, other incoming supply, and the reliability of the available information.

The Data Layer brings together these inputs and their update times. The Virtual System Layer relates them to the product–destination replenishment position and, where adequate inputs are available, assesses possible effects on inventory coverage and compares feasible responses.

The Governing Characteristics Layer specifies how data freshness, uncertainty, and the distinction between estimated arrival and confirmed product availability must be handled. The Service Layer presents the assessment, information limitations, and response alternatives to responsible personnel. Where important inputs are stale or missing, the assessment is presented as provisional and requires verification.

The framework’s intended scope extends to the overall COE.

Dashed relationships express architectural influence, including subsequent review during the DT lifecycle. The Physical System lies outside the Digital Twin boundary.
